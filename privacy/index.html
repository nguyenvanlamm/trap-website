01 / Read first

What this
policy covers.

This policy covers the current Trap mobile game, which is in development, this website and the public GitHub support forms managed by the Trap maintainers.

No account needed to play

Your progress and settings are stored locally. The game does not send gameplay data to servers operated by the Trap maintainers.

02 / Inside the game

Progress stays
close to the run.

The current game has no player accounts, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases or tracking. It stores the following information on your device so you can continue playing and keep your preferences.

Stored by the appWhy it existsCurrent handling
Unlocked levels and recordsResume progress and show deaths, times, batteries and ranks.Local preferences/save data on the device.
Challenge goals and viewed hintsRemember challenge completion and one-time guidance.Local until progress is reset or the app is removed.
Language and accessibility choicesKeep English/Vietnamese, reduced motion and left-handed controls consistent.Language and settings survive a progress reset.
Audio settingsRemember music, SFX, haptics and volume choices.Local preferences for the current app install.

A platform backup or restore may retain preferences. The maintainers cannot promise that uninstalling the app erases every backup copy; platform behavior controls that.

03 / Around the website

Two public surfaces,
different rules.

Landing website

This static site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP address and request information under its own policies when serving a page. This site adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, third-party trackers or automatic gameplay collection.

GitHub support issues

Choosing a support form takes you to a public issue in the Trap website repository. GitHub requires an account to submit it. The issue may show your GitHub username, attachments and anything you write; GitHub controls that service under its own privacy statement. Do not put passwords, personal data, identifiers or sensitive logs in a public issue.

The maintainers use information you choose to submit to investigate problems, respond to questions and consider improvements. Issues and comments may remain publicly available after they are closed. You can edit or delete your own comments using GitHub’s controls; contact GitHub through its privacy statement for questions about data it holds.

04 / Your choices

Reset what is yours
to reset.

  • Use the game’s progress-reset control when you want to clear local progress. Language and other settings are designed to remain.
  • Do not submit a support issue if the details cannot be public; redact sensitive logs instead.
  • For a GitHub issue, use GitHub’s own account and privacy controls for requests about your username or repository content.
  • There is no Trap gameplay account, mailing list or email-based support intake on this site.

05 / Questions

Questions about
your data?

For questions about this policy or the game’s data handling, contact the Trap maintainers using the feedback form on Trap Support. This is a public channel: keep your question general and do not include personal or sensitive information.
