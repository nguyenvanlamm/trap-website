How to play

Slow down.
Then go.

Two move buttons on the left. Jump on the right. Scan and EMP sit beside it. Hold jump for a higher hop; release early for a short one.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Remember where the last trap fired, or use Scan before you move. Hidden traps look like harmless scenery.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Spend a battery to scan a risk, or two to freeze a cluster. The charge is a decision, not decoration.

  3. 03

    Commit

    Move through the route you have earned. After the power core, hesitation gives the crusher time to close in.

What is Trap?+

Trap is a hidden-trap platformer about a small robot escaping a scrap factory. The room is designed to surprise you once, then reward you for remembering.

How much game is there?+

The current design has 12 levels across three acts: Scrap Yard, Maintenance Halls and The Grinder. There are 13 trap types and three challenge goals per level.

What happens when a trap gets me?+

You respawn at the last checkpoint you touched. A trap that has fired leaves a faint mark where it hid, and that discovery survives death.

When should I use Scan or EMP?+

Scan costs one battery and reveals hidden traps within 5.5 cells. EMP costs two and freezes nearby mechanisms for 2.6 seconds. Passive geometry—like an exposed spike bed—stays dangerous.

When can I play?+

Trap is in development. It is planned for Android and iOS, with both the Google Play and Apple App Store releases marked coming soon. No release date has been announced.
